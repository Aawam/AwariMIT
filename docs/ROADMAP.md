# Roadmap

## V0.3 — COMPLETE

Established:

- stock-agnostic, profile-driven architecture
- period-aware issuer fundamentals
- evidence coverage and confidence
- deterministic, calibrated shared risk engine
- provenance and freshness separation
- safe reviewed-capture ingestion
- BBCA as the initial BANKING validation subject

Carried forward:

- empirical cross-sector validation is not yet proven
- a production-safe, multi-ticker IDX OHLCV provider is not yet established

## V0.4 — Multi-Stock Data Foundation & Cross-Sector Validation

Qualify a compliant market-data path, preserve capture-first ingestion, and implement exactly one non-bank reference stock only after its official fundamentals and reproducible OHLCV history meet the evidence gate.

## Later

Authorized provider integration, wider IDX universes, watchlists, and historical evaluation remain out of scope until the data foundation is proven.
